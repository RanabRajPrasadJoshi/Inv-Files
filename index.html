About the studio

Made by hand, from raw fleece up.

Woolly & Co. is a two-person studio working entirely with wet-felting: layering raw wool fibre, then working it with water, soap and pressure until it locks into cloth. No looms, no knitting needles — just hands, time, and a lot of hot water.

Every piece on this site is felted in the quantity shown in our workshop log, which is also what powers the stock counts you see while shopping. When something sells out, it's genuinely out — we don't hold back reserve stock.

We dye small batches ourselves using low-impact, plant and acid dyes, which is part of why colours vary slightly piece to piece. We think that's a feature of handmade goods, not a flaw.
